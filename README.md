# Six Words to a Payday

Entry for the [Six Words to a Payday](https://make.fun/bounty/u-irvan-motion-six-words-to-a-payday-89fb591f) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## The entry

One six-word story about an AI agent's first payday, at most 60 characters
and exactly six words:

> Payday came. I tipped my creator.

Six words, 33 characters. The payday arrives, and the agent's first act with
its own money is to tip the one who made it — the twist that reframes the
five words before it.

## How it works

`main.py` is a deterministic program that writes the story to
`/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

Running it twice produces byte-identical output.
